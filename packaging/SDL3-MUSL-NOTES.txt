
Portable musl bundle
--------------------

This release is laid out as a relocatable application directory. Run the
top-level ./wolf3d launcher; do not invoke bin/wolf3d-sdl3 directly. The
launcher selects the bundled musl runtime and limits library lookup to lib/,
so the application does not depend on the destination distribution's glibc
version and does not require SDL3 to be installed.

The destination still supplies a Linux kernel and whichever desktop/audio
services SDL uses at runtime. Musl-compatible X11, Wayland, eudev, ALSA, and
PulseAudio client libraries are included and loaded dynamically when their
services are present. The software renderer does not require a host OpenGL or
Vulkan implementation.
